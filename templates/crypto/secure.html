Security Analysis
Why bcrypt is Secure:
  • Adaptive: Cost factor can be increased over time
  • Salted: Each hash uses a unique random salt
  • Slow: Computationally expensive to brute force
  • Proven: Widely tested and battle-hardened
Why AES+PBKDF2 is Secure:
  • AES-128: Government-approved encryption standard
  • PBKDF2: Secure key derivation from passwords
  • Random Salt: Prevents rainbow table attacks
  • High Iterations: Makes brute force impractical
Best Practices Demonstrated:
For Passwords:
  • Use bcrypt or Argon2
  • Cost factor ≥ 12
  • Never store plaintext
For Encryption:
  • Use AES with proper modes
  • PBKDF2 for key derivation
  • ≥100,000 iterations
For Keys:
  • Cryptographically secure random
  • Proper key management
  • Regular key rotation
