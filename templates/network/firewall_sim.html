Firewall Rule Simulation

What is it? Firewalls control network traffic by applying rules that determine whether to allow, deny, or log specific packets based on various criteria.

Rule Components:
  • Action: ALLOW, DENY, or LOG the traffic
  • Source/Destination: IP addresses or networks
  • Ports: Specific ports or port ranges
  • Protocol: TCP, UDP, ICMP, or others
  • Priority: Order of rule evaluation
Best Practices:
  • Default deny policy (implicit deny)
  • Most specific rules first
  • Regular rule review and cleanup
  • Comprehensive logging
  • Change management processes
