Real-World Examples
Coffee Shop Attack

Scenario: Attacker sets up fake Wi-Fi hotspot "Free_Coffee_WiFi" in a busy café.

Method: Victims connect to fake network, all traffic routed through attacker's device.

Result: Banking credentials, emails, and social media accounts compromised.

DNS Hijacking

Scenario: Attacker compromises router or DNS server to redirect legitimate websites.

Method: DNS responses modified to point to attacker-controlled servers.

Result: Users unknowingly submit credentials to fake banking sites.

BGP Hijacking

Scenario: ISP or nation-state attacker manipulates internet routing protocols.

Method: Traffic for major websites rerouted through attacker infrastructure.

Result: Large-scale surveillance and data interception.

Detection Techniques
Technical Indicators:
  • Certificate warnings or mismatches
  • Unexpected network latency or routing changes
  • Suspicious SSL/TLS cipher suites or protocols
  • Multiple DHCP servers on the same network
  • ARP table inconsistencies
Behavioral Indicators:
  • Slower than usual internet connections
  • Unexpected redirects or pop-ups
  • Sessions being logged out frequently
  • Unusual account activity notifications
  • Modified website content or layouts
